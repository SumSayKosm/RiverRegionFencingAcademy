Who We Are

About the Club

Our mission, values, and the code of conduct that guides everything we do.

Statement of Purpose

Our Mission

"Our mission is bringing people together to learn, practice, and enjoy fencing in a fun and supportive environment."

1

Friendly & Welcoming

Our mission is to create a fun, inclusive space where people of all skill levels can enjoy the art and sport of fencing, build confidence, and connect with others in a supportive community.

2

Community-Focused

We aim to foster a welcoming fencing community that encourages learning, camaraderie, and personal growth through friendly practice and shared passion for the sport.

3

Accessible & Encouraging

Our mission is to make fencing accessible and fun for everyone. We strive to be a safe, supportive environment where everyone is welcome. Club members can develop their fencing skills, stay active, and enjoy the sport at their own pace.

Community Standards

Code of Conduct

01

Respect for Everyone

  • Treat all members, guests, and instructors with respect and courtesy.
  • Harassment, discrimination, or disrespectful behavior of any kind will not be tolerated.
  • Encourage and support fellow fencers, regardless of skill level.
02

Safety First

  • Follow all safety rules and instructions at all times.
  • Wear appropriate protective gear during fencing activities.
  • Immediately report unsafe conditions, injuries, or equipment issues.
  • Fence with control and consideration for your partner.
03

Sportsmanship

  • Practice honesty and fairness in all bouts.
  • Accept victories and defeats gracefully.
  • Resolve disagreements calmly and respectfully.
04

Inclusivity

  • Welcome fencers of all backgrounds, identities, and abilities.
  • Help create an environment where everyone feels comfortable participating.
  • Avoid language or behavior that excludes or intimidates others.
05

Participation & Attitude

  • Come ready to learn, have fun, and contribute positively to the group.
  • Be open to feedback and willing to help others improve.
  • Keep a positive, low-pressure atmosphere.
06

Equipment & Facility Care

  • Treat club equipment and facilities with care.
  • Return borrowed gear in good condition.
  • Clean up after yourself and help maintain a tidy space.
07

Responsibility

  • Follow club policies and instructions from organizers or coaches.
  • Respect shared time and space.
  • Communicate if you have concerns or conflicts.
08

Accountability

  • Members are expected to uphold this code at all times.
  • Repeated or serious violations may result in warnings or removal from club activities.
